# Docs

- [Welcome to Paragraph](/): Paragraph gives writers a site, a newsletter, and a subscriber list they own, plus an agent that helps with the rest.
- Getting started
  - [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart): Create your account, meet your agent, and publish your first post.
  - [Import your publication](/getting-started/import-your-publication): Bring your posts and subscribers to Paragraph from Substack or another platform.
  - [Workspace tour](/getting-started/workspace-tour): A quick tour of the main areas in your Paragraph workspace.
- Your agent
  - [Meet your agent](/agent): Your Paragraph agent drafts, distributes, and maintains your publication through one-time suggestions and scoped automations.
  - [Chat with your agent](/agent/chat): Ask your agent to draft, research, schedule, and analyze, all from a conversation in your workspace.
  - [Voice and memory](/agent/voice-and-memory): Teach your agent your goals, brand voice, social voice, visual brand, and context so everything it makes sounds and looks like you.
  - [Inbox](/agent/inbox): Review one-time proposals from your agent and follow today's runs in one place.
  - [Automations](/agent/scheduled-tasks): Create jobs that run before publishing, after publication events, or on a schedule.
  - [Approvals](/agent/approvals): Understand the difference between choosing a suggestion once and authorizing an automation to act on a saved trigger.
  - [Connections](/agent/connections): Connect X, Telegram, Slack, and other tools so your agent can post, chat, and pull context from where you work.
- Writing and publishing
  - [Editor](/publishing/editor): Write, format, and publish posts in the Paragraph editor.
  - [Newsletters](/publishing/newsletters): Send your posts to subscribers by email, with deliverability handled for you.
  - [Your website](/publishing/website): Every publication gets a website, with a default design or a custom site your agent builds for you.
  - [Custom domains](/publishing/custom-domains): Point your own domain at your publication instead of its paragraph.com address.
  - [SEO and site maintenance](/publishing/seo): How your site stays fast, findable, and healthy, with daily upkeep from your agent.
- Grow your audience
  - [Subscribers](/audience/subscribers): Manage your subscriber list, import it from anywhere, and export it whenever you like.
  - [Distribution](/audience/distribution): Turn each post into X threads, LinkedIn versions, and newsletter sends, without doing it all by hand.
  - [X Articles](/audience/x-articles): Publish a long-form Article on X from Paragraph, review it before it goes out, and know which formatting carries over.
  - [Analytics](/audience/analytics): Understand how your posts, newsletter, and channels perform, in the app or by asking your agent.
- Account and billing
  - [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits): What each Paragraph plan includes, what it costs, and how credits power your agent.
  - [Manage your account](/account/manage-account): Account settings, publication details, automations, and taking your data with you.
- Developers
  - [Build with Paragraph](/developers): Integrate Paragraph into your application with the REST API, TypeScript SDK, CLI, MCP server, and agent skills.
  - [TypeScript SDK](/developers/sdk): Install the Paragraph SDK and query publications, posts, coins, and user profiles with full type safety.
  - [CLI](/developers/cli): Manage posts, publications, subscribers, and coins from the command line. Built for humans, agents, and CI.
  - [MCP server](/developers/mcp): Connect AI agents to Paragraph via the Model Context Protocol.
  - [Agent skills](/developers/agent-skills): Installable skills that teach any AI agent how to use the Paragraph API, CLI, SDK, and MCP server.
